# 千珠落尽 · 作画提示词（给 ChatGPT 生图用）

用法：每张图先粘「通用风格」，再粘这张图自己的那一段。提示词用英文，生图模型对英文更稳。
画好后把 PNG 按「文件名」一栏命名发我，我负责压缩、裁切、接进游戏。

---

## 通用风格（每张都先粘这段）

```
Flat vector illustration in a strict Art Deco style, for a premium dark-and-gold card game.
Palette: antique gold (#D9B45A and #B8913E) and near-black (#0D0B09) only, plus at most ONE small accent color named in the prompt.
Bold geometric shapes, strong symmetry, thin crisp gold outlines, flat fills, no gradients (or only a very faint one), no texture noise.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no signature. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime.
Solid near-black (#0D0B09) background. Subject centered with a clear margin around it.
Match this reference description: hooded and armored figures drawn as gold line-art and flat gold shapes on black, like a 1920s Art Deco poster.
```

## 小贴士

- 一次只生成一张，生成后如果出现文字或乱码字母，补一句 `Remove all text.` 重新生成。
- 同一个首领的两阶段，第二张生成时可以把第一张图一起发给 ChatGPT，说 `Same character as the attached image, now in its second phase:` 再接第二阶段的提示词，前后会更像同一个角色。
- 想整组风格统一，可以先画一张满意的，之后每张都附上它，加一句 `Match the style of the attached image exactly.`
